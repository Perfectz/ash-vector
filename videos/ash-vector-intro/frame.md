# ASH VECTOR cinematic identity
Concept: a cold industrial approach becomes a confrontation between one small operative and an enormous siege engine.
Palette: ink #091317, ash #eeeade, ember #f4653c, secondary #adc2c8. Reuse the game's physically based material palette for 3D objects and lighting.
Typography: Archivo Black 400 for the title; IBM Plex Mono 400/700 for field markings.
1920x1080, 20 seconds. 84px safe edge anchors. Title 180px. Supporting text 26px. Sharp corners. No UI screenshots or stock assets.
Focal elements: 3D world, operative, then mech. Supporting labels remain in a lower corner until the closing title takes the frame. Cinema bars are intentional compositional mattes. Closing image remains visible, no black ending.
Motion: pure-time camera poses via HyperFrames Three.js adapter; restrained cut rhythm; title scale settles with GSAP.
